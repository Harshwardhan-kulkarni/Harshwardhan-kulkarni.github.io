My story

Hi, I'm Harshwardhan. 23, ex robotics engineer, batch of '25.

In my final year I built a Stewart Platform to help autonomous drones land in diverse environments. It was the coolest project I've made. It's also how I realised I hated ROS and debugging for hours. (This was right before LLMs could vibe-code. Great timing, I know.)

I went into B2B sales instead, and realised I loved it. Cold calls, demo bookings, then project management at a niche manufacturing company, working directly with the CEO and clients.

Then I ran a category myself at Decathlon while trying to figure out "internet money". I burnt out trying to learn content on the side and went quiet for five months.

I'm back on Instagram, growing, learning content, GTM, and X (which I've barely used). Who should I be following?
